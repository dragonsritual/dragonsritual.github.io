# DragonsRitual Web Pass 05 — Town Foundation

This pass establishes the first public community/town architecture and a review-only Supabase migration for profiles, earned standing, badges, recurring programs and community proposals.

Principles: visitors may browse/use public utilities; registration creates continuity and participation; a person is distinct from a space/business/show; reputation is earned and cannot be purchased; paid advertising is disclosed; governance always declares who has decision authority; private creative content is not silently collected from anonymous tool sessions.

Do not run the new migration in production until authentication, RLS, moderation, privacy/retention, upload security, abuse controls and legal review are ready.
